1. Our Core Values

CareerCatalyst was built to be a safe, constructive haven for engineering students, fresh graduates, and career changers. We celebrate curious minds, transparent feedback, and shared learning. We expect every member to uphold three foundational values:

  • Mutual Respect: Every engineer was once a beginner. Treat fellow learners with patience, dignity, and empathy regardless of college tier, experience level, or background.
  • Constructive Guidance: When reviewing another student's resume or project code, offer specific, actionable suggestions rather than generic criticism or condescension.
  • Honesty & Transparency: Share genuine placement experiences, real interview insights, and authentic code implementations without exaggeration.

2. Discussion & Code Review Standards

When participating in our community marketplace and discussion boards:

  • Stay on Topic: Keep questions focused on engineering concepts, 12-week roadmap milestones, technical interview preparation, and placement navigation.
  • Format Code Cleanly: Use markdown syntax highlighting for code blocks so fellow students can read and review your work easily.
  • Search First: Before asking common questions (e.g. "How do I prepare for Python OOP?"), check existing roadmap guides to avoid repetitive threads.
  • No Self-Promotion or Link Dumping: Do not post personal YouTube channels, paid Telegram/WhatsApp study groups, or affiliate referral links.

3. Mentorship Etiquette

Our platform connects students with experienced industry engineers who volunteer their time to guide the next generation:

  • Respect Mentor Time: Come prepared to mentorship interactions with specific questions, clear project links, and your latest ATS report. Do not expect mentors to write code for you.
  • Professional Communication: Keep all communication strictly professional, courteous, and career-oriented.
  • No Job Solicitations: Do not pressure mentors for direct internal referrals or job offers during introductory guidance sessions. Referrals are earned through demonstrated technical excellence and established rapport.

4. Prohibited Behavior

Zero Tolerance Policy

The following conduct results in immediate content removal and account suspension or permanent termination:

  • Harassment & Bullying: Personal insults, targeted attacks, trolling, stalking, hate speech, or discrimination based on gender, caste, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or college pedigree.
  • Commercial Advertising & Spam: Selling paid courses, promoting third-party bootcamps, advertising resume-writing services, or distributing unauthorized marketing links.
  • Malicious Code & Exploits: Sharing links containing malware, phishing URLs, exploit scripts, or unauthorized web scrapers.
  • Impersonation: Falsely claiming to be a recruiter from a specific company, impersonating CareerCatalyst staff, or using another student's identity.

5. Academic Integrity & Company NDAs

We enforce strict standards regarding recruitment confidentiality and academic honesty:

  • No Leaked Live Assessment Questions: Do not post proprietary, copyrighted, or active coding test problems that violate company non-disclosure agreements (NDAs). Discussing general algorithmic patterns (e.g. "The round tested Breadth-First Search on a grid") is welcome; copy-pasting active proprietary problem statements is strictly prohibited.
  • No Cheating During Proctored Practice: Our technical quiz platform uses client telemetry (tab-focus tracking and countdown timers) to simulate authentic placement screening. Attempting to bypass proctoring tools defeats the purpose of honest diagnostic practice.

6. Moderation & Enforcement Tiers

Community moderators and system administrators apply progressive enforcement based on the severity of violations:

  1. Informal Warning: For minor first-time infractions (e.g., miscategorized threads or accidental link sharing), the post is moderated with an explanatory note.
  2. Content Removal & Strike: Repeated offenses result in immediate post deletion and a formal strike against the user profile.
  3. Temporary Account Suspension: Three strikes or serious misconduct lead to a 7-to-30 day suspension of community and workspace posting privileges.
  4. Permanent Termination: Severe violations (hate speech, harassment, spam bot campaigns, or security exploits) result in immediate permanent account termination and IP banning.

7. How to Report Violations

Maintaining a healthy, safe community is a shared responsibility. If you encounter abusive comments, spam, or suspicious activity:

  • In-App Flagging: Click the Report or flag icon next to any community post or message.
  • Email the Moderation Team: Send details, screenshots, and URLs to community@careercatalyst.in.
  • Confidentiality: All violation reports are treated with strict confidentiality. The reported user will not be informed of who submitted the report.
