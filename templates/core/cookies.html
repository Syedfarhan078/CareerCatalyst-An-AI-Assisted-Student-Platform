1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, smartphone, or tablet when you visit a website. They are widely used to make web applications function efficiently, preserve login sessions, remember user interface preferences, and safeguard platform security.

2. How We Use Cookies & Storage

CareerCatalyst uses cookies and local browser storage (localStorage) strictly for essential functionality, security verification, and basic aggregated platform telemetry:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for keeping you logged in securely, navigating between dashboard pages, and defending against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks.
  • Functional Preferences: Remembering your chosen color mode (Light vs Dark theme), sidebar drawer collapse states, and cookie banner consent status.
  • Aggregated Anonymous Telemetry: Counting anonymous page visits to understand which 12-week roadmaps (e.g., Cybersecurity vs Full Stack) are most in-demand, allowing us to allocate curriculum research resources.
  • Zero Third-Party Advertising: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, retargeting pixels (such as Meta Pixel or Google Ads tracking), or cross-site data aggregators.

3. Specific Cookies & LocalStorage Keys We Set

Name / Key Category Duration Purpose
sessionid Strictly Necessary 2 Weeks / Session Maintains your authenticated student or mentor session across workspace pages.
csrftoken Security 1 Year Protects forms and API endpoints from Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks.
theme_mode / theme Functional Persistent Preserves your chosen Light or Dark theme preference across visits.
cc_cookie_consent Functional Persistent Remembers your cookie choice (Accepted/Rejected) so the banner is not displayed on every page load.
