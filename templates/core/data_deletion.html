1. Instant Self-Serve Deletion

CareerCatalyst gives registered students and mentors a one-click self-serve mechanism to permanently delete their account and wipe all personal data:

Irreversible Action

Deleting your account is permanent. Once confirmed, all your roadmap progress, earned verification certificates, ATS analysis histories, and tracked job pipelines are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.

{% if user.is_authenticated %} Proceed to Delete Account {% else %} Sign In to Delete Account {% endif %}

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sign in to your CareerCatalyst account.
  2. Click on your avatar in the left sidebar or top right and navigate to Settings.
  3. Select the Delete Account tab or visit this direct deletion page.
  4. Enter your account password to authenticate and confirm your decision.
  5. Your account and all associated records are permanently erased, and you are signed out.

3. Zero Residual Retention

Unlike commercial platforms that "soft-delete" records or move accounts into an archival retention silo for 90 days, CareerCatalyst executes an immediate hard delete. No shadow profiles, backup clones, or user identity logs are maintained once deletion executes.

4. Manual Deletion Request (DPDP Act, 2023)

If you cannot access your account (e.g., lost credentials or expired session), you have the legal right under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 to submit a manual Data Erasure Request directly to our Grievance Officer:

  • Email: supportatcareercatalyst@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: [DPDP Erasure Request] Full Name - Registered Email
  • Required Information: Please send the email from the exact email address registered on the platform, along with your registered username.
  • SLA & Confirmation: We verify the request within 24 hours and permanently purge all associated records within 72 business hours, providing written email confirmation.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I recover my account after deletion?

No. Deletion is irreversible. If you wish to use CareerCatalyst again in the future, you will need to register as a new user.

What happens to public community forum posts?

Any community discussion posts or questions you authored are either deleted or anonymized (author shown as "Deleted Member"), ensuring no personal identifiers remain.

Is there any fee or charge to delete my data?

Never. All data rights, including access, correction, and permanent erasure, are 100% free of charge under our principles and the DPDP Act, 2023.
