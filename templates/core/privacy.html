Our Privacy Commitment

CareerCatalyst is an educational technology initiative built by engineering students for engineering students and freshers. We believe career development tools must respect candidate privacy. We do not sell your resumes, run intrusive third-party ad trackers, or share your profile with third-party headhunters without your explicit consent.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of CareerCatalyst ("we", "us", or "our") regarding the collection, use, retention, disclosure, and protection of personal data when you access or use our website (careercatalyst.in), learning roadmaps, ATS resume diagnostics, mock interview prep, Career Assistant, and job application pipeline tracking (collectively, the "Platform").

By creating an account, accessing the platform, or submitting information to CareerCatalyst, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and consented to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and applicable information technology regulations.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide personalized milestone curricula, resume benchmarking, and placement tracking:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full name, email address, password hash (salted and hashed using PBKDF2/SHA-256), and optional profile avatar.
  • Academic & Student Data: College or university name (e.g., Atria Institute of Technology), engineering branch/department, year of graduation, CGPA or percentage, and degree type.
  • Career Direction & Goals: Target engineering role (e.g., Software Engineer, DevOps, Data Scientist, QA Tester), target company preferences, and self-reported technical competencies.
  • Resume & Professional Content: Raw resume text, parsed resume sections (Education, Experience, Projects, Skills, Certifications), work history, achievement bullet points, and uploaded PDF/DOCX files.
  • Job Pipeline CRM Records: Applications you manually add to your Kanban board, including target company names, role titles, application dates, interview schedules, notes, and recruiter follow-up statuses.
  • Assessment & Performance Telemetry: Technical quiz responses, MCQ scores, coding challenge submission logs, test session timers, tab-switch or blur events recorded during proctored practice sessions, and roadmap milestone completion records.
  • Technical & Session Logs: IP address, device user-agent, operating system, browser type, and authentication session timestamps collected solely for platform security, fraud prevention, and session continuity.

3. Public Quick-Scan Zero-Retention Policy

Try-Before-Signup ATS Quick-Scan

Our homepage features an interactive "Try Before Signup" ATS Quick-Scan tool. When you paste resume text into this tool without logging in, the text is evaluated entirely in-memory using local Python taxonomy and TF-IDF routines. The text is never written to our database, never stored to disk, never logged to server files, and discarded immediately after the diagnostic JSON payload is returned to your browser.

4. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data for the following specific purposes:

  1. Personalized Curriculum Delivery: Customizing 12-week roadmap milestones and recommended topics aligned with your verified skills and target engineering career.
  2. Deterministic ATS Resume Scoring: Benchmarking your resume against industry role taxonomies, detecting missing domain keywords, and scoring bullet points against Google's XYZ achievement formula.
  3. Interview Preparation: Generating balanced practice question sets, assessing algorithmic problem-solving, and providing personalized skill-gap feedback.
  4. Application Organization: Maintaining your personal Kanban job tracker, computing timeline milestones, and alerting you when 5-day recruiter follow-ups are due.
  5. Account Administration & Security: Sending transactional verification OTPs, password reset emails, account recovery notices, and maintaining platform integrity.

6. Zero Sale of Student Data

We never sell, rent, broker, or monetize your personal data. Your resume, contact details, academic scores, and application lists are never sold to recruitment agencies, data brokers, advertising networks, or lead generators.

7. Storage & Security Architecture

We enforce multi-tiered defense-in-depth measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • Encryption in Transit: All HTTP traffic is strictly forced to HTTPS using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3/1.2) with strict HSTS headers.
  • Credential Protection: Passwords are never stored in plaintext; they are hashed using Django's PBKDF2 algorithm with SHA-256 iterations and random salts.
  • PII Sanitization: Our AI integration layer automatically scrubs telephone numbers, emails, and personal URLs before passing prompts to external LLMs.
  • Document Access Control: Uploaded resume files are private; access requires session authentication and ownership verification, served via short-lived expiring URLs.

8. Your Rights Under India's DPDP Act, 2023

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Indian citizens and platform users possess the following enforceable data rights:

  • Right to Access & Summary: You have the right to request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Updation: You can edit your profile, update contact information, change college details, and modify resumes directly at any time.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You have the right to demand the permanent deletion of all personal data, resumes, and accounts we maintain.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent for future data processing at any time by closing your account.
  • Right to Nominate: In the event of death or incapacity, you have the right to designate an individual to exercise data rights on your behalf.

9. Account & Data Erasure Procedure

We believe deleting your data should be just as easy as creating an account. You have two convenient options to exercise your right to erasure:

  1. Instant Self-Serve Deletion: Log in to your account, open Settings → Delete Account, or visit Account Deletion. Confirming your password immediately and permanently purges your user profile, resumes, analyses, applications, and attempts from our database.
  2. Manual Erasure Request: If you cannot access your account, submit a deletion request to our Grievance Officer at supportatcareercatalyst@gmail.com from your registered email address. We will verify your identity and purge all associated records within 72 hours.

For complete step-by-step instructions and an itemized breakdown of purged assets, see our dedicated Data Deletion Documentation.
