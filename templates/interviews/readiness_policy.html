1. Assessment Format & AI Involvement

  • Three Stages: Applied Diagnosis (Stage 1), Scenario & Constraint Pivot (Stage 2), and Bounded Adaptive Technical Defense (Stage 3).
  • AI-Era Evaluation Policy: Stage 1 presents flawed AI-generated engineering artifacts for your independent diagnosis. You are evaluated on defect judgment and reasoning, not copying external AI output.
  • Candidate Code Safety: Submitted code patches are inspected structurally without running untrusted code on platform servers.
  • Provider Resilience: If AI services fail during an assessment, your saved work pauses cleanly without failure penalties or cooldowns.

2. Integrity Telemetry & Privacy

  • No Webcam Surveillance: New Career Readiness sessions do not require camera monitoring or biometric tracking.
  • Lightweight Audit Telemetry: Sessions record stage lock timestamps, tab-blur counts, and large paste bursts solely to flag severe anomalies for audit—never to auto-fail a candidate.
  • Private Records: Drafts, stage transcripts, scores, and AI usage logs remain strictly private to you and authorized platform review.
